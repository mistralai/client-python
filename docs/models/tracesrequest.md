Trace fields (e.g. `service_name`, `error_count`, `models_used`) are evaluated on the aggregated trace. Span attribute fields (e.g. `temporalWorkflowID`, `wf.type`, `gen_ai.provider.name`) are evaluated on the spans of the trace: a trace matches when one of its spans matches. Span attribute conditions combined with AND must hold on the same span, e.g. `temporalWorkflowID = 'wf-1' AND wf.type = 'activity'` only matches traces with a span carrying both attributes. Results are always one row per trace. |
